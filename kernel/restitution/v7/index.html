Restitution is not a life fraction

Example: a parcel is withheld for ten years and then returned. The remedy is not automatically 10% of the parcel's market value. A complete assessment identifies return of possession and title, net lost use, any deterioration, and other distinct consequential losses.

Trace the original rightsholder

Muscogee land, 1832

Trace treaty entitlement, original land rights, transfers, later titles and actual payments. Land, loss of use, performance of treaty duties and restoration are separate records.

Enslaved persons and extracted wealth

Trace actual labor taken, property and opportunities denied, assets or proceeds transferred, the benefiting parties and succession. Do not substitute an owner's historical appraisal of a human being for the value of their rights.

Test of completion

A claim is not closed because a dollar value was calculated. Mark it fulfilled only after title/rights are restored where applicable, verified payments or other remedies are received, overlaps are reconciled, and the affected rightsholder or authorized successor has an opportunity to contest unresolved components.
