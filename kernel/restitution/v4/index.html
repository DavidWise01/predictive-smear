Three accounting states

RECOVERED — Federal enforcement reports back wages recovered. Do not charge those same dollars again.

SECURED — Court-approved settlement or agreement states an amount; payment completion may need verification.

UNKNOWN — Claims without identified principal, accrual dates, applicable interest law or payment receipts.
